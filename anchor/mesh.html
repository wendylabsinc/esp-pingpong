Boards and the distances between them

A solid line is a distance that at least three recent rounds agree on, and those lines are drawn to scale. A dashed line is the best guess so far. A faded line has had no new round for 30 seconds. Dotted lines join the anchor to the boards its radio hears; it measures no distance to them. Sound gives distances only, so the picture may be turned or mirrored against the room.

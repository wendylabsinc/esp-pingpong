Tap locator

Map (metres, from above)

Solid lines join boards that hear each other's sweeps: the distance that gives, then how well their clocks agree. A long-dashed line joins boards whose clocks agree by radio beacons or calibration taps, with no distance measured. Hollow boards have not been placed by hand and sit where the sweeps put them; a ring shows where the sweeps put a placed board. Dashed lines are each board's link to this box: latency ± jitter. The shaded region around the newest tap is where it lies with 95% confidence.
